Quantum Harmonic Oscillator Simulator for Quantum Computation

Encode two logical qubits in the energy eigenstates of a single quantum harmonic oscillator. At τ = ωt = π, free time evolution gives only the occupied odd level |1⟩ a relative phase of -1, implementing CNOT.

Energy-Level Populations

Free time evolution changes only phases, so the level populations remain constant. This encoding does not use |3⟩.

Physical limitations: This model reproduces the ideal encoding and free time evolution in Eqs. (7.13)-(7.15) of the textbook. Wavefunction and fidelity visualizations are additional features. It does not model hardware for arbitrary initial-state preparation, measurement, or sequences of arbitrary gates, and is not a practical, general-purpose quantum computer. Realizing a state evolution that implements a computation is not, by itself, sufficient to make a practical quantum computer.
